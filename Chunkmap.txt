🗺️ Atlas Development Roadmap — Chunk-Based Architecture

The overarching philosophy I'd use from here:

Build interconnected systems together, and prioritize anything that makes Atlas faster, more reliable, or more enjoyable to use before giving her greater autonomy.

Goal

🎯 Performance Goal

Eventually aim for roughly:

Simple interaction:
< 1–3 seconds

Normal interaction:
~3–10 seconds

Complex reasoning:
~10–20 seconds

Heavy analysis:
Background task

Not because those numbers are sacred, but because Atlas should spend expensive inference only where it actually provides value.
